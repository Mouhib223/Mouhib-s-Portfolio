About Me

I'm Mouhib Hlaili, a software engineer based in Tunis with hands-on experience across full-stack development and quality assurance. I build web applications end-to-end — from .NET and ASP.NET Web API backends to Angular and React frontends — and I take quality seriously at every step.

I care about delivering reliable software — not just software that works on my machine. That means writing clean, testable code, automating regression early, and communicating clearly with the people around me.
